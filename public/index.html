Test Instructions

Important Guidelines:

  • You will need TWO devices for this test (e.g., computer + phone)
  • Your main device will display the test and record your interpretation
  • Your second device (proctoring device) will provide an additional camera angle
  • Both cameras must be active throughout the entire test
  • You will interpret from English into your target language
  • Maximum test duration: 35 minutes
  • You may request up to 5 interventions without penalty
  • Maximum 10 interventions allowed per test
  • All recordings are automatically uploaded to prevent data loss

Intervention Types:

  • Request Repetition: Ask the speaker to repeat the last segment
  • Request Research Pause: Take up to 90 seconds to look up terminology
